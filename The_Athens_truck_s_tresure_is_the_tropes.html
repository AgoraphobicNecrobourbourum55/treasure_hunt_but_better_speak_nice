Hidden treasure game

The screen on this truck shows the Periodic Table of Storytelling. Somewhere among its tropes a chest is buried. Light up the table and dig it out.

Treasure in the tropes Ten shovels. One chest.

Move the light over the table. A glint means you are close. Click or tap to dig, and the meter tells you how warm you are. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys and Enter.
